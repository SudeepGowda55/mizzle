Breathe in. They'll know.

Right before you talk, you take a quick breath without noticing. Mizzle sees it through your webcam and tells everyone on the call you're about to speak, so you stop talking over each other.

  • Stays on this laptop. Video and breathing are processed here. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
  • Only a status leaves. Your name and quiet / about to speak / speaking go to people in your room through a public relay.
  • Camera is on only in a room. Leave the room and it switches off. Presage SDK telemetry is turned off.
  • Not medical. It reads breathing rhythm for conversation, nothing about your health.
